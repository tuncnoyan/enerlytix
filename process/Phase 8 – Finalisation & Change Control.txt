Phase 8 – Finalisation & Change Control
Goal: Introduce governance and report locking.

Features:

1. Final Status:
- Draft
- Validated
- Final

2. Locking
Final reports become: "Read-only" for:
- Owner
- Team Lead
- Manager

3. Reopen Request:
3.1. Workflow:
Owner
  ↓
Request Edit
  ↓
Team Lead
  ↓
Approve / Reject

Manager can also approve.

4. Audit
Record:
- Requested by
- Approved by
- Date
- Reason
